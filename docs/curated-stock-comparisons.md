# Curated PSX comparison pages

Fifty-three focused sector pairs across 36 selected companies and ten sectors are defined in `src/lib/stock-comparisons.ts`. Thirteen original pairs retain their canonical URLs and remain featured on the main hub. Additional pairs use deliberately bounded company sets; there is no all-market or cross-sector pair generation. All three FATIMA/FFC/EFERT combinations remain covered. Sector membership here is a comparison grouping, not a claim that the companies have identical business models or that no other companies are listed.

Each pair has server-rendered company names, market and valuation rows, sector-specific fundamentals, individual reporting periods, source links, breadcrumbs, a unique title/description and a self-canonical URL. Quotes come from PSX DPS; detailed fundamentals and full-year cash dividends come from the existing hourly-cached SCSTrade company snapshots. Annual yield is full-year DPS divided by the displayed quote. The fiscal year and cash DPS are shown; yields are withheld when available fiscal years differ. There is no fallback to PSX yield because its payout window is unspecified. Calculated price/book uses the PSX quote and positive reported book value. Unknown values stay missing. No financial winner, forecast or investment recommendation is generated.

Canonical pages are built statically and revalidated every five minutes on access. Feed caches retain their existing refresh policies. Reverse order, case variants and a small set of explicit company-name aliases permanently redirect to the canonical pair. Unsupported pairs return 404 rather than generating arbitrary pages.

All canonical pairs appear in the XML sitemap and the crawlable `/stocks/compare/all` directory. The main hub features only 13 selected pairs and a directory link. Related lists on individual comparison pages are capped at six links. Participating stock pages provide server-rendered links to their curated comparisons, outside the client-side research tabs. Generic comparison customization still supports query-string stock selection.

Schema uses WebPage/Corporation references and BreadcrumbList only; it does not claim a supported comparison rich result, rating or guaranteed ranking. Google ultimately controls indexing and search presentation.

Validation: all 36 symbols had PSX quotes and book values during the source coverage audit. The SEO verification checks that every generated pair has a directory link and sitemap URL, alongside unique metadata and canonical checks. Existing reporting-year and dividend alignment guards remain active; this audit checks availability, not independent certification of every upstream financial value.
