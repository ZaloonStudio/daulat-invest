Delete your Daulat account or data

Daulat: Stocks & Portfolio is provided by Zaloon Studio.

How to request deletion

  1. Email ZaloonStudio@gmail.com from your registered account email.
  2. Use the subject Daulat account deletion. Specify whether you want your entire account deleted or only particular holdings, trades or watchlist records.
  3. We verify account ownership before carrying out the request. Never send your password, Google sign-in codes or Gemini API key.

What is deleted

For a verified full-account request, we delete the Daulat authentication account, associated account-link records, and cloud watchlist, portfolio holdings and trade-history records. You can also remove individual holdings, trades and watchlist entries in the app.

Local records and retention

Cloud deletion does not erase copies already saved on your own devices. Clear local app storage or uninstall Daulat to remove those copies. Signing out alone does not delete cloud data. Daulat does not impose an additional retention period on your account records after verified deletion. Technical service logs and provider backups follow the providers' applicable security and retention policies. Public company and market information is not personal account data and is retained.

If you used Gemini with your own key, requests sent to Google are governed by Google's terms and retention policies. This request does not delete your Google account.
