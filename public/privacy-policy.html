Daulat Privacy Policy

Your financial privacy is our utmost priority • Zaloon Studio

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal financial data or portfolio holdings to anyone. Period.

1. Information We Collect (and Do Not Collect)

Daulat does not request bank credentials, CNIC numbers or broker trading PINs. Optional account features collect your email address, Google profile name (when provided) and account identifiers. Signed-in cloud sync stores the watchlist symbols, portfolio holdings and trade records you enter, including quantities, purchase and sale prices and dates. Guest holdings and watchlists are stored on your device.

2. Smart Screenshot Importer & Image Processing

Screenshot import uses Google ML Kit to recognise text on your device. Daulat does not upload the selected broker image for OCR. Holdings that you choose to import can be synced to Firebase if you are signed in. ML Kit sends installation identifiers, device/app information, feature-use events, performance metrics and error codes to Google for diagnostics and usage analytics; it does not send the image contents for text recognition.

3. Cloud Sync & Account Data

Google or email sign-in uses Firebase Authentication to process your email, account identifiers and authentication information, and your Google profile name when provided. Firebase also processes IP addresses and technical information for security and abuse prevention. Firebase Firestore stores your signed-in watchlist, holdings and trade records for backup and cross-device sync. These account and financial records are retained until you remove them or request deletion; signing out does not delete cloud records.

4. Service Providers & Optional AI

We use Google Firebase for authentication and cloud storage, and Google ML Kit for on-device OCR with technical diagnostics and usage analytics. We do not include advertising SDKs or sell your data. If you connect your own Gemini API key and request an AI answer, your question, relevant stock information and profile first name may be sent to Google Gemini. Official company filings may also be sent for summaries. Google processes these requests under its applicable terms. Avoid entering sensitive personal information in AI questions. Online services receive network and device information needed to deliver their services; app service requests use HTTPS.

5. Data Deletion & Control

You can remove individual holdings, trades and watchlist entries in the app. To request deletion of your Daulat account and associated Firebase data, email ZaloonStudio@gmail.com from your registered email with the subject Daulat account deletion. Include the account email, but never your password. We use that information to verify ownership and process the request. Signing out or uninstalling alone does not delete cloud records.

6. Contact Us

For any inquiries or suggestions concerning this Privacy Policy, please email Zaloon Studio at ZaloonStudio@gmail.com.
